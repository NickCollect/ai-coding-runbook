# The Societal Impacts team studies how AI affects people, both individually and collectively. We measure Claude’s effect on how people think, work, and relate to one another, and use what we learn to improve our models and products.

![Video thumbnail](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/4zrzovbb/website/4f268223739d63a56f89b21a45559ba82392e8e0-1280x720.jpg)

Anthropic Insights dataset
:   [Visit](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Anthropic/enabling-independent-research)

### Measuring real-world impact

How does AI affect people’s wellbeing, reasoning, and relationships? Our researchers study how people interact with Claude at scale, and interview them directly about their experience. We publish what we learn, and work to support outside researchers studying these questions.

### Improving our models and products

Our research identifies ways Claude can serve people better. We act on what we learn by integrating these findings into changes to Claude’s behavior, new evaluations, and product improvements.

[## What 81,000 people want from AI](https://www.anthropic.com/features/81k-interviews)

We invited Claude.ai users to share how they use AI, what they dream it could make possible, and what they fear it might do. Nearly 81,000 people participated—the largest and most multilingual qualitative study of its kind. Here's what we found.

[Read more](https://www.anthropic.com/features/81k-interviews)

[## Enabling independent research on how people use Claude](https://www.anthropic.com/research/enabling-independent-research)

We ran a pilot giving external researchers access to aggregate, real-world Claude usage data. In this post, we share high-level results and what we learned running this pilot.

[Read more](https://www.anthropic.com/research/enabling-independent-research)

[## Measuring AI agent autonomy in practice](https://www.anthropic.com/research/measuring-agent-autonomy)

We analyzed millions of human-agent interactions across both Claude Code and our public API using our privacy-preserving tool, to ask: How much autonomy do people grant agents? How does that change as people gain experience? Which domains are agents operating in? And are the actions taken by agents risky?

[Read more](https://www.anthropic.com/research/measuring-agent-autonomy)
