Gemini 4 Argon is Google's frontier Gemini model. Use it for complex, multi-step coding work that benefits from deeper reasoning and a large context window.

## Effort levels

Gemini 4 Argon supports four effort levels: high, medium (the default), low, and minimal. Raise it to high for harder tasks, or drop to low or minimal for faster responses on simpler ones.
