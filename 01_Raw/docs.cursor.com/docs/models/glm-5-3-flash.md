GLM 5.3 Flash is the smaller, faster member of Z.ai's GLM 5.3 family. It supports image inputs, a 1M-token context window, and three reasoning levels (Low, High, and Max), with High as the default. Add it to the model picker from **Cursor Settings > Models**. For higher quality on harder tasks, use [GLM 5.3](https://cursor.com/docs/models/glm-5-3.md).

## Hosting

GLM 5.3 Flash is an open-weight model from Z.ai. Cursor serves it through our inference partners. Learn more about [where models are hosted](https://cursor.com/docs/models-and-pricing.md#faq) or see the full list of [sub-processors](https://trust.cursor.com/subprocessors).

## Pricing

GLM 5.3 Flash is a third-party model, so it isn't part of the usage included with Cursor [plans](https://cursor.com/docs/models-and-pricing.md#third-party-models). On The X Subscription it's billed at the rates below from your prepaid credit. On legacy Cursor plans it draws from the **Other Models** pool. All prices are per million tokens.

Pricing is flat across the full context window, with no long-context surcharge and no separate cache-write fee.
