Gemini 4 Neon is Google's Gemini model for agentic coding and the successor to [Gemini 4 Hydrogen](https://cursor.com/docs/models/gemini-4-hydrogen.md). Use it for everyday agentic coding and tool-heavy work. For the hardest tasks, use [Gemini 4 Carbon](https://cursor.com/docs/models/gemini-4-carbon.md).
